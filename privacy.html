Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Movie Friend helps you find someone in your city who wants to see the same film, at the same show, then meet at the theatre. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and who can see it. It is written to describe what the app actually does — not a template.

The short version: your phone number is never shown to anyone. A work or college email, if you choose to verify one, is never stored as readable text and never shown to anyone. Safety features — reporting and blocking — are free and available to everyone, and a report is confidential: the person you report about is never told who reported them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party analytics inside the app.
  • We do not track your location in the background or continuously — your city is set once, not streamed.
  • We do not show your phone number, or a verified work/college email, to any other user, ever.

Who we share information with

The app runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, and the server-side functions that verify a work email and moderate reports). Google processes this data on our behalf, under its own terms, to provide that infrastructure — it is not shared for its own purposes.

Film listings — titles, posters, runtimes, certificates — come from TMDB. This flows one way, into the app; nothing about you is sent to TMDB.

If you tap "Book tickets," you leave the app for BookMyShow or a similar ticketing site to complete your purchase. We do not see your payment details, and their own privacy policy governs that part of the visit.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from the You tab in the app ("Delete account"), or by emailing admin@moviefriend.in if you no longer have the app installed. This removes your profile, any blocks, any pending match requests, and any reports you filed or were named in. Chat messages and arrival check-ins tied to your past matches are not deleted, since they are also part of the other person's own conversation history — but with your profile gone, nothing in them can be traced back to identify you.

Age

Movie Friend is for adults. The app requires you to confirm you are 18 or older when you set up a profile, and it is not directed at children.

Security

Data in transit and at rest is protected by Firebase's own encryption. Access to your profile data by other users is limited to what matching genuinely needs — see the table above. Server-side rules, not just the app itself, enforce who can read and write what.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and the update will be described here.
