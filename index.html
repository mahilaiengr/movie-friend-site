Movie Friend

Why watch alone?

Movie Friend helps you find someone in your city who wants to see the same film, at the same show — then meet at the theatre.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your phone number. It is never shown to anyone.
  2. Browse the films currently showing in your city.
  3. Mark the shows you would go to.
  4. Get matched with people going to the same one, and agree on seats.

Trust

People can verify a work or college email address to earn a Verified professional or Verified student badge. The address itself is never stored in readable form, and verification is a signal — not a requirement to join. Reporting and blocking are available to everyone and are never placed behind a paywall.
